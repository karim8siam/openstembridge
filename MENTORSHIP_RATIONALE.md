# Research collaboration design rationale

The 30-day local-only collaboration charter is informed by university mentoring guidelines, not a substitute for institutional research agreements.

- Purdue University, Office of Undergraduate Research, *Best Practices in Mentoring Undergraduate Student Researchers*: agreement on expectations, milestones, communication and development. https://purdue.edu/undergrad-research/faculty/resources/best.php
- MIT UROP, *Expectations for the UROP Experience*: expectations documents should be revisited; discuss project deliverables, weekly milestones and work style. https://urop.mit.edu/mentors/resources/urop-experience/
- University of Minnesota Office of Undergraduate Research, *Mentoring Tips & Guidelines*: student responsibilities, research approvals, communication and advice. https://ugresearch.umn.edu/mentors/mentoring-tips-guidelines
- University of Colorado Boulder UROP, *Create a Mentor Agreement on One Page*: goals, schedule, learning, expectations and compliance. https://www.colorado.edu/urop/engaging/faculty-grant-acceptance/mentoring-guide/reflect-plan/create-mentor-agreement/create
- Council on Undergraduate Research, *Mentoring in Undergraduate Research*: time commitment, communication, outcomes and feedback. https://www.cur.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Mentoring_in_UR__CUR.pdf

**What the product changes:** it asks professors for mentorship and time details before any opportunity can be published, routes applicants through consent-aware moderation and encourages a shared kickoff charter. It does not certify mentorship quality or perform university-specific legal, labor, IRB or ethics checks.

**Competitor signal:** ForagerOne is used by higher education institutions to list research projects with qualifications, compensation arrangements, weekly hours and project timelines (example institutional instructions: https://www.morgan.edu/office-of-undergraduate-research/faculty-resources/foragerone). This supports the desirability of structured calls, not evidence that OpenSTEM has product-market fit or institutional customers.
