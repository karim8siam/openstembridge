Is OpenSTEM Match open to students globally? +

Yes. The platform welcomes students and independent researchers worldwide. Introductions depend on mutual research fit, supervisor time, and project constraints.

What makes a student application stand out? +

Demonstrated evidence: a runnable GitHub repository, reproducibility report, or clean simulation. A small, well-documented project speaks louder than GPA or prestige alone.

How does the introduction process work? +

Dual-consent is strictly enforced. When an applicant expresses interest or a researcher requests a student, our team confirms both parties' availability and consent before exchanging direct contacts.

Can research labs post funded or volunteer positions? +

Yes. Every brief transparently indicates whether the role is funded, credit-earning, or volunteer, alongside expected hours and mentorship support.

How is privacy protected? +

Student and researcher emails are never displayed in public listings or API endpoints. Profiles only appear publicly if the applicant explicitly opts in.
